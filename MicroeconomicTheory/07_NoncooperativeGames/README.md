# MWGNoncooperativeGames

Formalization of Mas-Colell, Whinston and Green, *Microeconomic Theory*, Chapter 7, "Basic
Elements of Noncooperative Games", pp. 219-234: what a game is and the examples of Section 7.B,
the extensive form (7.C) with information sets, perfect information and perfect recall,
strategies and the normal form (7.D), and mixed and behaviour strategies (7.E) with Kuhn's
theorem, which the book cites, proved in both directions. Every in-text and end-of-chapter
exercise (7.C.1, 7.D.1, 7.D.2, 7.E.1(a)-(d)) is formalized. The audit passed **183 theorems
and 301 declarations across 10 modules**. Coverage is in the [source map](docs/source-map.md);
no item is OPEN. Departures from the printed text are in [corrections](docs/corrections.md).

This is a Lake project pinned to Lean and Mathlib `v4.34.0`; it requires Mathlib only.
See [third-party notices](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md).

## Design note: the representation used by later chapters

Chapters 8, 9, 12, 13, 14 and 23 import this library. The representation is fixed here.

* **Games in extensive form** are `ExtensiveForm I A H`: players `I`, actions `A`, and
  information-set labels `H`. A node is a **history**: the list of actions leading to it from
  the initial node, *most recent action first* (`[]` is `x₀`, `a :: x` is the successor of `x`
  by action `a`, `p(a :: x) = x`). `nodes` is a finite set of histories closed under `tail`;
  `info : List A → H` is `H(·)` on decision nodes, `avail k` is `C(k)` (the field `avail_spec`
  is MWG's "same choices in one information set"), `player k : Option I` is `ι(k)` with `none`
  for nature, `natureProb` is `ρ`, and `payoff i z` is `u_i` at terminal nodes. Recursion over
  the tree is structural recursion on lists. `GameTree` is MWG's abstract tree
  (items (i)-(iii) on an arbitrary finite node set), and `GameTree.hist` with
  `hist_injective`, `cons_mem_historyNodes_iff` and `ExtensiveForm.toGameTree` show that the two
  descriptions are interchangeable.
* **Perfect information and perfect recall** are `PerfectInformation`, `NoAbsentMindedness`
  (footnote 1, condition (i)), `RecallFor i` (condition (ii) for player `i`) and
  `PerfectRecall`, all quantified over the finite node set so that `decide` checks them on
  concrete games.
* **Pure strategies** are `PureStrategy G i := (k : InfoSetOf i) → {a // a ∈ avail k}`
  (`Fintype` when `A`, `H` are finite with decidable equality); `pureOfFun` builds one from a
  plain function.
* **Randomization** is a real vector: move probabilities `f : H → A → ℝ`. `reach f x` is the
  probability of reaching `x`; `assemble F` combines the players' plans `F i` with nature;
  `pureProfile s`, `IsBehaviorStrategy`, `IsMixedStrategy` (any finite type), `mixedOutcome`.
  `sum_reach_terminals` shows every profile induces a probability distribution on terminal
  nodes; `reach_assemble_factor` and `mixedOutcome_factor` factorise it across players.
* **Normal forms** are `NormalForm I S` with `payoff : I → ((i : I) → S i) → ℝ`;
  `ExtensiveForm.toNormalForm` gives the expected-utility payoffs of pure profiles,
  `NormalForm.mixedPayoff` and `mixedExtensionGame` the mixed extension, and
  `mixedPayoff_toNormalForm` identifies the two expected utilities.
* **Kuhn's theorem**: `RealizationEquivalent`, `kuhn_behavior_to_mixed`,
  `kuhn_mixed_to_behavior`, `kuhn_theorem`, `kuhn_mixed_rivals`.

## Build and verify

From this chapter directory:

```sh
lake build
python3 -m unittest discover -s scripts -p 'test_*.py' -v
python3 scripts/verify.py
```

The verifier builds the library, recompiles all sources in a fresh file, and audits every
declaration's axioms (only `propext`, `Classical.choice` and `Quot.sound` are allowed).
